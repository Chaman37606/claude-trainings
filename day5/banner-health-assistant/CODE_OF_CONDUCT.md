# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project — filing issues, opening PRs, reviewing, discussing — to
be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or
identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in feedback; critique the code and ideas, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Ask clarifying questions before assuming bad intent.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and expect a reason when they do.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or review.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces — issues, PRs, code review comments, and any other
project-affiliated communication.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the repository owner (see repository contact information). All
reports will be reviewed and addressed as promptly as reasonably possible. Maintainers who do
not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent
repercussions as determined by other project leadership.

This document is intentionally short — it borrows its structure from the widely-used
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) without reproducing it verbatim.
