ALCOA Plus

Data Integrity Framework for Regulated Industries

What is ALCOA Plus?

ALCOA Plus is an enhanced framework that defines the essential characteristics of reliable, compliant data and records in regulated industries. It builds upon the original ALCOA principles by adding two critical components: Compliance and Auditability. This framework is essential for organizations operating under regulatory requirements such as FDA 21 CFR Part 11, ICH guidelines, and other international standards.

The Seven Principles

Original ALCOA

Attributable

Every record must clearly identify who performed an action, when it was performed, and why. Records should include user identification, timestamp, and purpose of the action.

Original ALCOA

Legible

All records must be clear, readable, and understandable. Data should be presented in a format that can be easily read and understood by intended users without ambiguity.

Original ALCOA

Contemporaneous

Data must be recorded at the time an event occurs, not retroactively. Records should reflect real-time activities and decisions made during the actual process.

Original ALCOA

Original

Records should be maintained in their original form. Any copies must be verifiable as true copies, and changes must be tracked without destroying the original data.

Original ALCOA

Accurate

All data must be correct, precise, and complete. Records should reflect factual information and include all relevant details necessary for decision-making.

ALCOA Plus Extension

Compliant

Records must conform to all applicable regulatory requirements and internal company policies. Systems and processes should be designed to meet regulatory standards from inception.

ALCOA Plus Extension

Auditable

Records must support inspection and audit procedures. Complete audit trails should be maintained to track all changes, access, and modifications to data.

ALCOA vs ALCOA Plus Comparison

Key Benefits

  • Ensures regulatory compliance and reduces audit risk
  • Enhances data quality and reliability
  • Improves traceability and accountability
  • Supports decision-making with trustworthy data
  • Facilitates inspections and regulatory submissions
  • Minimizes operational and legal risks
  • Builds stakeholder confidence and credibility
  • Enables quick root cause analysis and investigation

Industries & Applications

  • Pharmaceutical: Drug development, manufacturing, quality control, and clinical trials
  • Medical Devices: Design, validation, manufacturing, and post-market surveillance
  • Biotechnology: Research, testing, and validation processes
  • Healthcare: Electronic health records (EHR) and patient data management
  • Food & Beverage: Safety records and compliance documentation
  • Manufacturing: Quality assurance and production records
  • Financial Services: Transaction records and audit compliance

Implementation Best Practices

  • Implement validated Electronic Data Management (EDM) systems
  • Define clear roles, responsibilities, and access controls
  • Establish comprehensive audit trail capabilities
  • Document and validate all processes and procedures
  • Provide regular training to all personnel
  • Conduct periodic audits and assessments
  • Maintain detailed records of all data lifecycle events
  • Establish change management procedures
